3. Recipients and processors

  • The organizer of the respective event: receives the order data needed for entry and settlement.
  • Supabase (database, authentication, servers) – EU data centre (Ireland).
  • Stripe (payment processing and payouts to organizers).
  • Google Firebase (push notifications) and Google AdMob (ads).
  • Didit (age verification, only for age-restricted events).
  • Apple Wallet / Google Wallet – only if you add a ticket yourself.
  • Vercel (hosting of Creator Studio) and GitHub Pages (hosting of this website).
  • An email service provider for sending confirmations.

Data processing agreements are in place with all processors, or they act as independent controllers under the GDPR. Transfers to the USA rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses.

4. Retention

We keep account data as long as your account exists. Receipts and booking records must be retained for 7 years under Austrian law.

5. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection. You can withdraw consent at any time. Get a copy of your data in the app under Account settings → Export my data or by email.

You may lodge a complaint with the Austrian Data Protection Authority: www.dsb.gv.at.

6. This website

This website uses no cookies and no tracking. When you visit, the hosting provider processes technically necessary data (e.g. IP address) to deliver and secure the site. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.
