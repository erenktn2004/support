For organizers

Everything you need to sell tickets. Nothing you pay extra for.

From club gigs to home games with a grandstand: Milligate takes care of tech, payment and entry.

Seats, standing, parking

Stands with rows and seats, standing quotas or free seating – plus bookable parking in the same checkout.

Money straight to your account

Payments run through Stripe and are paid out to you automatically. No advance, no waiting for a statement.

Entry with the scanner app

Create scanner accounts for your team. Every ticket has a unique QR code – copies and double sales are caught instantly.

Live numbers & settlement

See what’s sold in real time and download a clean settlement after the event.

Refunds without the hassle

Buyers cancel in the app up to 24 hours before the event, refund included. Nothing for you to do.

Real age checks

For 16+ or 18+ events, Milligate verifies age before purchase – digitally, without you handling ID copies.
