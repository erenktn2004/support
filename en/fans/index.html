The app

Buying tickets as easy as online shopping

Pay your way

Card, Apple Pay or Google Pay – securely via Stripe. We never store your card details.

In your wallet

Save your ticket to Apple Wallet or Google Wallet with one tap – available offline too.

Pick your seat

Choose your seat on the seating plan or go standing – and add parking in the same checkout.

Cancel flexibly

Cancel in the app up to 24 hours before the event. Your money comes back automatically.

Pass it on

Bought for the whole group? Transfer single tickets to friends with a Milligate account.

Safe & genuine

Every event is reviewed and every ticket has a unique code. No fakes, no double sales.
